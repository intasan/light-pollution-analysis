# Light Pollution Analysis

## Overview

An environmental analysis project about artificial light at night and its effects on people, ecosystems and the night environment.

## Topics

- Excessive outdoor lighting
- Light trespass and glare
- Skyglow
- Impacts on sleep and daily life
- Impacts on nocturnal animals and ecosystems
- Energy waste
- Practical mitigation approaches

## Suggested analysis flow

```text
Problem -> Causes -> Effects -> Examples -> Mitigation -> Awareness
```
